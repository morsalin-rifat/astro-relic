# 📚 Resources Used — Astro Relic

Everything we used to build Astro Relic, for the NASA Space Apps judges.

## 🛰️ NASA data & history

| Resource | Used for |
|---|---|
| [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov) | Mission photos, Apollo surface photos, intro footage |
| [NASA Photojournal (JPL)](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov) | Rover photos (e.g. PIA00611 Pathfinder airbags) and Mars discovery cards |
| [Apollo Lunar Surface Journal](https://www.nasa.gov/history/alsj/) | Real Apollo quotes, timeline and landing-site details |
| [NASA Mars Exploration](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/) | Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity and InSight mission facts |
| [Mars Pathfinder / Sojourner](https://mars.nasa.gov/mars-exploration/missions/pathfinder/) | Ares Vallis mission story |
| [Mars Exploration Rovers](https://mars.nasa.gov/mer/) | Spirit at Gusev, Opportunity at Meridiani, final messages |
| [InSight Mission](https://mars.nasa.gov/insight/) | Elysium Planitia, marsquakes, last selfie |
| [NASA 3D Resources](https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov) | Rover and lander 3D models |
| NASA traverse maps & MOLA / LRO terrain | Shape of our procedural Mars and Moon landscapes |
| Apollo mission patches (NASA, public domain) | Moon patch ceremony and certificates |

## 🎨 Media made by the team

- **Scripts** — kid-friendly English & Bangla stories for every rover and Apollo mission.
- **Voices** — recorded English & Bangla voice clips (`public/audio/`); some lines still use text-to-speech.
- **AI-assisted images** — Nova the guide, spacesuit selfie backgrounds (Moon and Mars).
- **3D worlds** — all terrain, rocks, dust devils, footprints and stars are generated in code.
